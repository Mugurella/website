Homo Atechnicus

A playful rock song about our daily battles with technology.

❀ Listen Everywhere

Take this bloom with you.
Wherever you love listening, the music will be waiting.

❀ Behind the Bloom

Every one of us has experienced that little battle with technology.

A USB stick that refuses to connect. A cable that only works at a certain angle. A device with a mind of its own.

Instead of getting frustrated, I decided to smile.

Homo Atechnicus was born from those everyday moments we all know so well.
